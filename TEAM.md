## Roles

In alphabetical order.

### Developer

The Open Source Developer plays a vital role in the growth, stability, and evolution of DAWO-NixOS. As a (volunteer) contributor, this individual provides their technical expertise and creative problem-solving skills to improve the project’s codebase, documentation, and overall user experience. The primary goal is to contribute high-quality code or resources that adhere to project standards while collaborating effectively with the maintainer team.

#### Core Responsibilities

##### 1. Technical Contributions & Remediation

**Bug Identification & Reporting:** Actively use the software as an end-user to identify edge cases, regressions, and bugs. Provide clear, reproducible steps and diagnostic data (logs, screenshots) via the issue tracker.

**Code Contribution (Pull Requests):** Develop and submit Pull Requests (PRs) that address specific issues or implement requested features. This includes writing clean, efficient code that aligns with the project’s existing architecture and style guidelines.
Unit & Integration Testing: Write automated tests for new features and bug fixes to ensure regression-free deployments. Contribute to improving the overall test coverage of the repository.

**Refactoring & Optimization:** Identify opportunities to improve code readability, reduce complexity, or increase performance in non-breaking ways.

##### 1. Documentation & Content Creation

**Technical Writing:** Create, update, and refine documentation, including README files, API references, tutorials, and "Getting Started" guides to lower the barrier of entry for new users.

**Community Knowledge Sharing:** Participate in community forums or Discord channels to answer user questions, provide troubleshooting advice, and share knowledge with fellow contributors.

**Localization & Translation:** Assist in making the project accessible to a global audience by translating UI strings, documentation, or community resources into multiple languages.

##### 1. Collaboration & Community Engagement
 
**Peer Review Participation:** Provide constructive feedback on other contributors' Pull Requests, helping maintain high standards and fostering a collaborative development culture.

**Adherence to Governance:** Follow the project’s established contribution guidelines (CONTRIBUTING.md), communication protocols, and branching strategies.

**Active Feedback Loop:** Engage with maintainers during the review process to clarify requirements, address requested changes, and ensure that submissions meet the needs of the user base.

##### 1. Competencies & Attributes

**Technical Proficiency:** Demonstrated knowledge in the Nix language and a basic understanding of Git version control workflows (forking, branching, rebasing).

**Self-Directed Learning:** The ability to navigate unfamiliar codebases independently and proactively seek out areas where help is needed.

**Clear Communication:** The ability to explain complex technical problems in understandable terms for both fellow developers and non-technical users.

**Empathy & Patience:** A willingness to work within a community framework, accepting constructive criticism from maintainers and fostering a supportive atmosphere for other new contributors.

### Maintainer

The Maintainer serves as the primary architect, technical authority, and community steward for DAWO-NixOS. This role is responsible for ensuring the long-term health, security, and scalability of the codebase while fostering a healthy, inclusive environment for contributors. 

#### Key Responsibilities

##### 1. Technical Stewardship & Engineering Oversight

**Code Quality Assurance:** Review and merge Pull Requests (PRs) to ensure all new contributions adhere to established syntax standards, architectural patterns, and performance benchmarks.

**Bug Triage & Resolution:** Act as the primary point of contact for reporting critical issues. The Maintainer is responsible for diagnosing root causes, prioritizing fixes based on severity, and overseeing complex refactors.

**Security Governance:** Monitor for vulnerabilities, coordinate security audits, and oversee the rapid deployment of hotfixes to protect user data and system integrity.

**Technical Debt Management:** Periodically audit the codebase to identify and resolve technical debt, ensuring that the project remains maintainable as it scales.

##### 1. Community Engagement & Mentorship

**Contributor Onboarding:** Develop and maintain clear contribution guidelines (CONTRIBUTING.md). The Maintainer assists new contributors in navigating the environment and successfully shipping their first contributions.

**Moderation & Tone Policing:** Govern community spaces (Forgejo, Signal, etc.) to ensure discussions remain productive and respectful. They are responsible for filtering noise and de-escalating conflicts.

**Documentation Strategy:** Ensure that technical documentation remains accurate, accessible, and up-to-date. The Maintainer oversees the creation of tutorials, API references, and community guides.

**Public Relations:** Act as the primary spokesperson for the project, communicating updates, setbacks, and major milestones to the user base and the wider developer ecosystem.

##### 3. Strategic Roadmap & Vision

**Roadmap Development:** Define and communicate the long-term technical goals of the project. The Maintainer prioritizes features based on community demand, technical feasibility, and strategic alignment.

**Release Management:** Oversee the end-to-end release cycle, including versioning, testing across multiple environments, changelog generation, and deployment coordination.

**Ecosystem Growth:** Identify opportunities for integration with other tools/libraries, manage cross-project dependencies, and foster partnerships within the open-source community.

##### 4. Requirements & Qualifications

**Engineering Excellence:** Deep expertise in the Nix language and a proven track record of managing complex software architectures.

**Diplomatic Communication:** Superior ability to provide constructive feedback that encourages contributors rather than discouraging them.

**Strategic Thinking:** Ability to balance "quick wins" for the community with the long-term requirements of the codebase.

**Resilience:** A high degree of emotional intelligence and patience for navigating the unique challenges of public, volunteer-driven development.
